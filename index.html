About our publishing tool 關於我們的發布工具

Laugh in a Blink uses a small internal tool to schedule and publish our own short videos to our own accounts on Instagram, Facebook, YouTube and TikTok. The tool is used only by our team, only for accounts we own, and does not collect data from other users.
